Healthy Recipes for Real Schedules

These recipes are built for students: minimal ingredients, affordable, and flexible. Most can be prepped in 20 minutes or less.

Quick, Real-Life Cooking Inspiration

This short video highlights simple, whole-food ingredients that can be combined into fast, balanced meals for everyday life.

Overnight Oats (No-Cook Breakfast)

Base: Rolled oats + milk or non-dairy milk + chia seeds (optional).

Add-ins: Frozen berries, peanut butter, sliced banana, cinnamon.

Steps: Stir everything in a jar at night, refrigerate, and grab it in the morning.

Fast Recipe Ideas

  • Black Bean & Corn Quesadillas
  • Frozen Veggie Stir-Fry with Rice
  • Greek Yogurt “Dessert” Bowl

Sheet Pan Chicken & Veggies

Toss chicken pieces, chopped potatoes, and frozen veggies with oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Bake on a sheet pan until the chicken is cooked through.

  • Uses basic pantry spices
  • Great for meal prep (leftovers for 1–2 days)
  • Easy to swap veggies based on what’s on sale

Snack Ideas with Protein + Fiber

Pair a source of protein with something high in fiber to stay full longer:

  • Apple slices + peanut butter
  • Whole-grain crackers + cheese
  • Carrots or cucumbers + hummus
  • Greek yogurt + fruit + granola
