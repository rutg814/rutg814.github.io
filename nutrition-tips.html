Quick Nutrition Tips for Busy Days

These tips are meant to be realistic, not perfect. Pick one or two that feel doable this week and build from there.

Daily Habits That Add Up

  • Carry a water bottle and aim to refill it 3–4 times per day.
  • Add one fruit or vegetable to at least two meals each day.
  • Include a source of protein at meals (eggs, beans, chicken, tofu, yogurt, etc.).
  • Keep a simple snack in your bag so you’re not stuck skipping meals.
  • Eat slowly and check in with your hunger and fullness halfway through meals.

5-Minute Fixes

  • Swap soda for flavored sparkling water or diluted juice.
  • Use frozen veggies to instantly upgrade instant ramen or pasta.
  • Make a quick snack: fruit + nuts, yogurt + granola, or hummus + veggies.
  • Build a “snack plate dinner” with crackers, cheese, veggies, and fruit.

Eating Well on Campus

  • Scan menus for words like “grilled,” “baked,” or “roasted” instead of “fried.”
  • Ask for sauces and dressings on the side so you can control the amount.
  • Build a balanced plate: ½ veggies/fruit, ¼ protein, ¼ grains or starch.
  • Use your meal plan for bigger meals and keep snacks from home to stretch your budget.

Myths vs. Real-Life Nutrition

  • Myth: “Healthy eating is all-or-nothing.”
    Reality: Small changes over time matter more than one “perfect” day.
  • Myth: “You have to cut out carbs completely.”
    Reality: Carbs give you energy, focus on whole grains and portion sizes.
  • Myth: “Healthy food is always expensive.”
    Reality: Frozen veggies, beans, eggs, oats, and rice are budget-friendly staples.
