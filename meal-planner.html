Weekly Meal Planner

Use this simple planner as a starting point for your week. The goal is not perfection, it’s to have a loose plan so you’re not stuck eating vending machine food at 11 pm!

Sample Day on a Budget

Here’s a sample day of affordable, balanced meals that work with a busy schedule.

Meal Example Approx. Prep Time
Breakfast Overnight oats with frozen berries and peanut butter 5 minutes (night before)
Lunch Brown rice, black beans, salsa, shredded cheese, frozen veggies 10–15 minutes
Dinner Whole-wheat pasta, jarred tomato sauce, frozen spinach, chicken strips 15–20 minutes

Tip: Choose 2–3 breakfast ideas and 3–4 main meals to repeat through the week to save money and time.
